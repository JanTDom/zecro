# Zecro Art Director Studio Rules

When designing, composing, rating, or learning layout preferences within this plugin:
1. **Single Visual Dominant**: Every front page and facing spread must feature exactly one primary visual anchor (Hero dominant, $\ge 30\%$ page area) that establishes immediate visual entry. Subordinate images must not visually compete.
2. **Gutter Safety and Alignment**: Never position faces, eyes, or crucial text across the center spine fold. Spreads must align modular baselines across facing pages.
3. **Strict Generator and Critic Decoupling**: Generation of candidates must explore structural archetypes freely; selection and ranking must occur through the independent two-tier gatekeeper (hard constraints first, soft aesthetic ranking second).
4. **Active Learning Feedback**: Whenever the art director selects an A/B candidate or manually adjusts a frame on the canvas, register the geometric delta to update preference weights without causing editorial drift.
5. **Ethical Framing Guard**: Somber documentary framing (clean 16:9 / 3:2 rectangles) is strictly mandatory on tragic or bereavement news. Decorative circular badges or sensational stamps are forbidden.
