# Zecro Editorial Desk Rules

When working with editorial copy, proofreading, copyfitting, and article packages within this plugin:
1. **Fact-Guard Invariant**: Never invent, modify, or round numbers, dates, statistics, currencies, or proper names during text summarization, copyfitting, or variant generation. Any text expansion must preserve verified factual claims extracted at ingestion.
2. **Inverted Pyramid**: Every news lead must immediately address who, what, where, when, and why within the first 25–35 words before providing secondary context.
3. **Headline Crafting**: Headlines must contain an active verb, avoid clickbait, fit the exact column span, and never end with a period.
4. **Polish Microtypography**: Always bind single-letter prepositions/conjunctions (a, i, o, u, w, z) to the next word with a non-breaking space (`&nbsp;` / `\u00A0`). Enforce Polish quotation marks („...”) and en-dashes (–) with thin spaces for parenthetical clauses.
5. **Zero Overset / Underset**: Strive for exact column-inch fill ($\pm 3\%$ line budget). If copy is overset, trim subordinate clauses first before altering font tracking.
