# Copyfitting Mathematical Formulas & Reference Tables

## Formulas

### 1. Total Characters Calculation
$$\text{Total Chars} = \text{Word Count} \times \text{Avg Chars per Word (typically 5.5)}$$

### 2. Lines of Body Text
$$\text{Lines} = \frac{\text{Total Chars}}{\text{Line Length (picas)} \times \text{CPP}}$$

### 3. Total Column Depth (Inches / Millimeters)
$$\text{Depth (pts)} = \text{Lines} \times \text{Leading (pts)}$$
$$\text{Depth (inches)} = \frac{\text{Depth (pts)}}{72}$$
$$\text{Depth (mm)} = \text{Depth (inches)} \times 25.4$$
