# Newspaper Editorial Copyediting Skill

This skill provides editorial standards, headline formulas, and copyediting procedures for professional journalism.

## 1. Multi-Tier Headline Formulas

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ KICKER / OVERLINE: 1-3 words in bold sans-serif        │
│ "HISTORIC CLIMATE SUMMIT"                              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BANNER / HAMMER HEADLINE: Active present tense verb    │
│ "140 NATIONS SEAL BINDING PACT IN GENEVA"              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ DECK / SUBHEAD: Narrative summary (1-2 sentences)      │
│ "Envoys agree to enforceable decarbonization quotas    │
│ and establish a $450 billion transition fund."         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Headline Rules
1. **Active Voice & Strong Verbs**: Prefer *"Senate Passes Landmark Bill"* over *"Landmark Bill Passed by Senate"*.
2. **Present Tense for Past Events**: News headlines use present tense for recent events (*"Diplomats Sign Treaty"*).
3. **Omit Articles**: Drop "a", "an", "the" where clarity permits.
4. **No Period at End**: Headlines never end with a period; question marks or exclamation marks are used sparingly.
5. **Comma Replaces 'And'**: In traditional headlines, a comma can substitute for 'and' (*"President, Prime Minister Meet in Warsaw"*).

## 2. The Anatomy of a Photo Caption (Cutline)

A publication-grade cutline follows a **two-sentence rule**:
- **Sentence 1 (Present Tense)**: Identifies the subject, action, location, and date:
  *`"Delegates applaud inside the Palace of Nations in Geneva at dawn on Thursday after reaching unanimous consensus on global reduction targets."`*
- **Sentence 2 (Past/Context Tense)**: Provides background context or significance:
  *`"The accord concludes 72 hours of continuous deliberations."`*
- **Credit Line**: Mandatory suffix: `(Photo: Associated Press / Marcus Rivera)` or `(Fot. Jan Kowalski / PAP)`.

## 3. Dateline & Byline Standards

- **English (AP Style)**: `GENEVA — In a decisive breakthrough...`
- **Polish (PAP / Press Style)**: `GENEWA (PAP) — W przełomowym głosowaniu...`
- **Byline Formats**:
  - `By ELEANOR VANCE in Geneva`
  - `BY ARTHUR PENROSE • SENIOR FINANCIAL CORRESPONDENT`
  - `Tekst i zdjęcia: MICHAŁ ZIELIŃSKI`

## 4. Jump Lines (Story Continuation)
- Column bottom: `— Continued on Page A4, Column 2 —` or `— Dalszy ciąg na s. 4 —`
- Target page header: `— Continued from Page A1 —` or `— Dokończenie ze s. 1 —`
