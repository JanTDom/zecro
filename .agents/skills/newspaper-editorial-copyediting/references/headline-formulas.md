# Advanced Headline Types

1. **Hammer Head**: 1-2 oversized punchy words (e.g. `CEASEFIRE!`) with a 2-line deck underneath.
2. **Kicker Head**: Short categorical overline in small caps/sans preceding a serif headline.
3. **Tripod Head**: Two-column layout where a large hammer sits on the left, flanked by two stacked sub-headlines on the right.
4. **Drop Head / Read-Out**: A secondary headline that drops down from the main streamer into the first text column.
