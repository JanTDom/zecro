# Newspaper Layout Patterns & Archetypes

## 1. The Classic Broadsheet Front Page (The "New York Times" Archetype)
- **Top Center / Banner**: Elegant masthead in Blackletter / Old English (e.g., Chomsky / UnifrakturCook / Pirata One) flanked by ear boxes (Weather on left, Index/Price on right).
- **Above the Fold**: Dominant right-hand lead story with 3-column headline, large hero photo, and secondary left-hand lead story.
- **Below the Fold**: 3-4 column modular articles separated by delicate 0.5pt vertical column rules.

## 2. The Bold Tabloid Front Page (The "Daily Telegraph / Express" Archetype)
- **Hero Dominance**: Huge hammer headline (72pt–120pt sans-serif, e.g., Oswald / Anton / Inter Black), dramatic full-width or half-page photograph.
- **Punchy Teasers**: Bright kicker ribbon, bulleted deck summary, concise 2-column lead text jumping to Page 4.

## 3. The Opinion & Editorial Spread (Op-Ed)
- **Columnist Columns**: 3 to 4 uniform columns, each headed with an author portrait/sketch, author byline in small caps, and bio.
- **Pull Quotes**: Large center-aligned or floated pull quote breaking across columns with decorative top/bottom double borders.
- **Editorial Cartoon**: Dedicated 2-column framed box with artist credit.

## 4. Business & Financial Section
- **Market Ticker Header**: 1-row table/bar with DJIA, S&P 500, NASDAQ, FTSE, Gold, Oil, EUR/USD changes.
- **Dense 5-Column Grid**: Tighter leading, smaller body font (9.5pt on 11.5pt leading), data tables with alternating subtle rules.

## 5. Culture & Arts Feature
- **Asymmetric Grid**: 3 wide columns (e.g. 1.5fr 1fr 1fr), generous whitespace, elegant serif typography (Cormorant / Playfair), large full-bleed header image.
