HISTORIC ACCORD • GENEVA DIPLOMACY

Global Summit Reaches Binding Climate Agreement

Envoys from 140 nations ratify landmark emission caps following intense 72-hour negotiations; industrial transition funds established.
By ELEANOR VANCE in Geneva Special to Gazeta Codzienna

GENEVA — In a decisive diplomatic breakthrough that followed three days of round-the-clock deliberation, representatives from 140 sovereign nations ratified a binding international treaty aimed at stabilizing global industrial output and accelerating renewable infrastructure deployment.

The accord, finalized under the auspices of the International Environmental Council, establishes enforceable decadal targets alongside a dedicated 450-billion-dollar multilateral transition fund designed to support emerging economies.

“We have moved past declarations of intent to actionable, enforceable obligations,” declared lead negotiator Dr. Helena Lindqvist during a crowded press briefing. “This framework represents a generational consensus that economic growth and planetary stewardship can no longer be separated.”

Key provisions of the agreement mandate verified carbon accounting for maritime shipping, phased decommission schedules for unabated coal infrastructure, and sovereign green bond standards.
